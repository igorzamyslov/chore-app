Famdo keeps a household's chores and shopping in one friendly place.

* Recurring chores: every N days/weeks/months, weekday pinning, or "N days after last done"
* Fair rotation between family members, or fixed assignment, or free-for-all
* Skip, pause (vacations), undo — the schedule stays honest
* Who actually did it: completions are credited to the person acting
* Shopping list with categories, type-ahead suggestions and duplicate prevention
* One gentle daily summary notification — only when something is actually due
* English and German, light and dark, fully offline (your data stays on your device)
* Export your data as JSON any time

Free and open source (MIT). No ads, no tracking, no account required.
