Daily reminders now actually arrive every day. Before, they stopped after the first one until you reopened the app.

The app no longer shows yesterday's list if you leave it open overnight.

In a shared household your phone now knows who you are, so two phones can't credit different people for the same chore. Use "Mark done for..." to credit someone else.

Android auto-backup is now switched off, so your household data is never copied off the device automatically.
